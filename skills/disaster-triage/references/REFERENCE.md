# Severity Scoring Matrix

- **1 - Low:** Minor waterlogging, localized debris, no immediate danger to structures or lives.
- **2 - Moderate:** Road blockages, minor utility interruption, localized flooding below curb level.
- **3 - Elevated:** Rising floodwaters reaching entrances, contained brush fire near access roads.
- **4 - Severe:** Rapidly rising floodwaters, uncontained structural fire, cracked load-bearing walls. Evacuation mandatory.
- **5 - Critical:** Flash flooding, full building collapse, industrial hazardous gas leaks, trapped victims. Immediate emergency dispatch.
